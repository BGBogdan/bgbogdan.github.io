Privacy policy

PA database app · Last updated 22 September 2026

This policy explains what information PA database app accesses when you use it, and what happens to that information.

What PA database app is

PA database app is a private sign-in connection that lets people who have been given access use Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, to query a Google BigQuery database. It is not a public service and has no user accounts of its own.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • See your email address, to identify which Google account is signed in.
  • Access Google BigQuery on your behalf. Google describes this permission as "View and manage your data in BigQuery". What you can actually reach is limited by the permissions your Google account has in Google Cloud. The account used with this app is set up with read-only access, so it can read data but cannot create, change or delete anything.

How the information is used

The information is used only to run the queries you ask Claude to run and to return the results to you in Claude. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, it is not used to build profiles, and it is not shared with anyone other than the services listed below.

Services that handle your information

  • Google handles sign-in and runs the BigQuery queries, under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic stores the sign-in token so Claude can make requests for you, and receives the query results you ask for, under the Anthropic Privacy Policy.

What is stored

PA database app does not keep its own copy of your data. Google Cloud keeps standard logs of the queries that are run, including which account ran them and when. The owner of the Google Cloud project can see those logs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PA database app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can stop the app accessing your account at any time:

  • In Claude, go to Settings → Connectors and disconnect it.
  • In your Google account, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove PA database app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
